Host Ramadan Month

Thank Allah for being alive

Remember that this world is only a game. Whoever lives there perishes; situations change, and each servant will be questioned. Whoever clings to it will always be busy, it will not reach the end of what it wants, and will not get what it wants. The days and months of this world pass at an astonishing speed. Life is short and the end of the world is near. This world is full of dangers, temptations, small sins and big sins.

People live there by being believers or disbelievers, pious or debauched, winners or losers. But happiness will only be for him who has protected himself as well as his children, his wives, and those for whom he is responsible, for all that generates the wrath of Allah, which brings regret, and all means of evil and destruction:

Profit month has come to you. He is a brilliant visitor who brings innumerable benefits. During this month, we only see inclined worshipers, people who read the Quran while lowering themselves before Allah and crying. Thank Allah for having made you live until this month because how many people wanted to reach this month and have not reached it. Death surprised them and made them perish.

We have reached it and how many loved people have we lost? We fast and how many people dear to our eyes have we buried? Death is approaching us, the departure is near and we have no provisions! Let's hurry before we can no longer repent. Be determined, do good for Allah, because those who have succeeded have put in a lot of effort.

Know that the one who is always lazy will not realize his desires and will be a loser. Aïcha (radiallahu 'anha) said:" The Messenger of Allah made efforts during the month of Ramadan, as he did not work in other months. " [Reported by Muslim]

Redouble our efforts during this blessed month

The month of Ramadan is the month of acceptance, freedom from hell, benevolence, upliftment ... what a loss for those who are lazy! Anas (radiallahu 'anhu) said that the prophet (sallallahu' alayhi wa sallam) reports that Allah said:" When the servant approaches Me with a span, I approach him with a cubit. When he come close to Me with a cubit, I get closer to him a breaststroke. When he comes towards Me while walking, I run towards him (understand eagerly). "

Now is the time to make an effort if you want to make it. It is the time of worship if you are prepared for it because it is the period of acceptance, of good. The devil is chained and the door of good is open for those who wish to enter. The Messenger of Allah (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) said:" When the month of Ramadan arrives, the doors of Paradise are opened, the gates of Hell are closed and the devils are chained. » [Reported by al-Bukhari and Muslim]

Hurry to repent because the time of repentance has arrived, and Allah loves those who repent. One day, the Messenger of Allah (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) went up on the minbar and said:" [Amin - Amin - Amin]. Then they say to him,"O Messenger of Allah! You got on the mimbar and you said: [Amin - Amin - Amin]". He replied: The angel Djibril came to me and said:"Whoever fastes the month of Ramadan and is not forgiven for his sins, then enters hell, may Allah remove him!"Say: Amin". So I said,"Amin". " [Reported by Ibn Khouzéïma and Ibn Hibane]

O you who watch forbidden things while you are in the best of months, take care not to violate his sacred state, to tarnish his honor and to diminish his rank. The prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) said:" He who does not give up lying, Allah does not need that he leaves his food and drink. » [Reported by al Bukhari]

What happiness for those who left the bestial desires for the invisible of the other world which they did not see, but believed in it:

Devote yourself fully to the Quran

The best way to spend your time is by studying the Quranic verses and thinking about their meaning. The angel Djibril ('alayhi salam) met the Messenger of Allah (sallallahu' alayhi wa sallam) every night during the month of Ramadan to teach him the Quran. Al Quran is a healing for hearts, its stories are very wonderful, its beneficial sermons, its magnificent wisdoms and its proofs on the uniqueness of Allah are obvious. The Quran is the soul for eternal life and without a soul, the body dies.

Dig into the Quran and take out its pearls. Learn the causes of the descent of the verses and read the books of the Quran commentaries (Tafsir). Do not limit yourself in reading the Quran and make it your guide in all your affairs. Muslims will never be happy until they fully practice the Quran.

Spend for Allah

The prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) said:" The best alms are given during the month of Ramadan. " [Reported by Tirmidhi] Ramadan is the month of spending, giving and caring. You rich people think of the hungry, the poor and the destitute. Help them, enrich them, and feed them! Spend and do not be stingy or Allah will give you nothing. The Messenger of Allah (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) was the most generous of men, and he was even more generous during the month of Ramadan. And whoever gives a faster enough to break his fast, he will have the same reward as him without this in any way diminishing the reward of the donor.

Take the Souhour meal

The Messenger of Allah (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) said:" People will always be fine as long as they will hasten to break the fast. The difference between our fasting and the fasting of the people of the Book is the meal of the Souhour - before the morning prayer - Take the Souhour, even with a sip of water. »The prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) broke the fast before praying with ripe and fresh dates. If they weren't fresh, eat it anyway. If they weren't dates, he would just drink a few sips of water.

However, be careful not to break the fast before its precise moment. Also be careful not to break this sacred month by breaking your fast voluntarily without excuse. As for the one who eats or drinks out of oblivion, let him finish to fast normally because it is Allah who fed and watered him. He does not owe any compensation for this day of fasting. The same goes for those who vomit while fasting. He must continue as if nothing had happened. On the other hand, those who vomit voluntarily must replace this day with another.

Same for him who brings out his sperm voluntarily, in addition to incurring the wrath of Allah, his fast will be broken. Whoever sperm comes out while sleeping is not responsible, his fast is still valid.

Staying praying at home is better for women

When the woman goes out to go to the mosque to pray to the isha and tarawih, she should not wear perfume and wear clothes that attract men. Indeed, according to Zeïnab Ath-Thaquafiah (radiallahu 'anha) , the messenger of Allah (sallallahu' alayhi wa sallam) said:" When one of you performs the prayer of al 'isha at the mosque, she should not wear perfume. " [Reported by Muslim]

In addition, the prayer made at home is better for her because according to Ibn Omar (radiallahu 'anhuma) , the prophet has ( sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) said:" Don't stop your women from going to the mosque, but their houses are better for them. " [Reported by Abou Dawoud] . And according to Oum Salamah (radiallahu 'anha) , the Messenger of Allah (sallallahu' alayhi wa sallam) a says:" The best mosque for women is at home. " [Reported by Ahmed]

Don't waste food and drink

A bad habit has appeared among some Muslims, it is the waste of food and drinks during the month of Ramadan. Many of them throw the rest of the food into the trash, while there are hungry people and families looking to eat and drink. So, servants of Allah, fear Allah; this is not how you thank Allah for the benefits He gives you and that you can ward off misfortunes:

Multiply the requests to Allah

Every day Allah hears the requests of fasters during the month of Ramadan. According to Abu Saeed Al-Khudri (radiallahu 'anhu) , the prophet (sallallahu' alayhi wa sallam) said:" Every Muslim has a request addressed to Allah which is answered every day and every night. " [Reported by Al Bazzar] According to Amr ibn Al-Ass (radiallahu 'anhu) , the prophet (sallallahu' alayhi wa sallam) also says:" The faster has a request which is not rejected when he breaks the fast. » [Ibn Madjah]

Increase the invocations during the month of Ramadan: for you, your family and your loved ones. Raise your hands to heaven with humility and ask Allah to rescue your weak brothers and sisters, driven from their homes, afflicted and persecuted all over the world. Our community is suffering tremendously now and many conspiracies are being made against Muslims. We can only get out of it by completely relying on Allah and trusting him completely.
