The Goal of the month of Ramadan

The Benefits of Ramadan Fasting of the Month

L'objectif du visé durant le mois du Ramadan est d'atteindre la piété (at-taqwa), comme Allah nous dit dans le Coran au sujet du jêune :

Fasting paralyzes all members of the body against desires and temptations. It is a real shield against fire, evidenced by the words of the prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) said:" Fasting is a shield from Hellfire . » [unanimously recognized hadith]

It is not for nothing if the Messenger of Allah (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) ordered those who had intense desires but could not be marry to observe the fast which is for them a shield against these desires while awaiting marriage. [al Bukhârî and Muslim] Fasting is also a good way to remember the situation of the hungry among the needy. By restricting the passage of food and drink through the veins, Shaytan is weakened because Shaytan flows through the veins of Adam's son. In addition, on the physical level, this prevents the strength of the limbs (through fatigue) from submitting to illicit activities which cause him harm in this life here below and that of the beyond. >

The practice of fasting the prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) was the most perfect of practices and the best in order to achieve the goal of fasting, godliness. To strive for this perfection It is therefore important to be interested and to know how fast the Messenger of Allah (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) . Of all the actions, fasting is the best with Allah. However, the faster does nothing except to abandon his desires, his food and his drink for his Lord. But fasting is precisely that, it is the abandonment of the love of the soul for the love of Allah. It is a secret between the worshiper and his Lord because fasting is not an act that is seen as prayer, zakat ...
