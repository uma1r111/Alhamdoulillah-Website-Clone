What is deprecated in Prayer

The Acts to Avoid When We Pray

There are things to avoid doing in your salat. Although doing them does not cancel the prayer, it is not recommended. In Arabic, this is called makrouhatou assalat, which is makrouh (hateful) in prayer.

  1. Playing with clothes or body
  2. Raise his eyes to the sky
  3. Watch what distracts
  4. Praying with a meal when you are hungry
  5. Pray with desire to go to bed
  6. Pray drowsiness
