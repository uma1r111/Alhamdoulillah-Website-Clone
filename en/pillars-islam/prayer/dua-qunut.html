Dua Qunut

Invocation of Qunoot

Doua al qounout means an invocation that is recited during prayer when standing. It is advocated when the witr prayer by straightening up the incline. This invocation is recommended when Muslims are affected by ordeals. During the month of Ramadan, the invocation of the Qounout is done all over the world, almost every day during al witr prayer.

Doua al Qounout during al Fajr

There is no authentic record that the Prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) did so regularly. What is sure, however, is that he was the qounout during periods of calamity. For example, during Fajr's prayer, he has already done doua Raal, Dhakwan and Oussayya because they had killed Koran bearers (in their hearts) sent by the Prophet sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam to transmit and learn religiion. In the other direction, he was also doua al qunout for weak and oppressed believers. But it was temporary each time, for specific events, it was not a habit.

Still in this sense, a hadith authenticated by al Albani and reported by the 5 (except Abu Dawood), Abu Malik Malik al-Achdja'i said: " I said to father: dad, you prayed behind the messenger of Allah (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) , Abu Bakr,' Omar, 'Outhman and Ali (radiallahu 'anhoum) . Did they used to do the qounout during the soubh prayer? His father then replied, "Son, this was invented later.

What is the formula of the qunout doua?

There are several invocations of the qounout reported in the hadiths. That said, you can also invoke Allah with other words. As for the question of whether to say the doua before or after the roukou, most of the hadiths indicate that one does doua al qounout after the inclination. But there is no harm in doing it before the ruku ', after finishing his recitation and before saying "Allah Allah".

Last point, the hadith narrated by Muslim, according to Jabir, where the prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) said: " The greatest merit of prayer lies in the length of the qounout. Here, qunout does not mean doua al qunout, but standing posture. According to Imam-Nawawi, this is the unanimous opinion of the Ulemas.

Dua Qunut 1

The dowry of the prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam) taught Hassan ibn Ali. The hadeeth is reported by Abu Dawood, an-Nassai and authenticated by al Albani.

اللهم اهدني فيمن هديت ، وعافني فيمن عافيت ، وتولني فيمن توليت ، وبارك لي فيما أعطيت ، وقني شر ما قضيت ، فإنك تقضي ولا يقضى عليك ، إنه لا يذل من واليت ، ولا يعز من عاديت ، تباركت ربنا وتعاليت ، لا منجى منك إلا إليك

« Allahouma ihdini fi man hadayt, wa 'afini fi man 'afayta wa tawallani fi man ta wallayt, wa barik li fi ma a'tayta, wa qini sharra ma qadayt, fa innaka taqdi wa la youqda 'alayk, innahou la yadhillou ma walayt, wa la ya'izzou man adayt, tabarakta rabbana wa ta'alayt, la mandja minka illa ilayk. »

O Allah, guide me with those whom you guide, give me security with those to whom You give it, take me in charge with those whom You take charge of, bless for me what You have given me, protect me against Your worst decisions, You judge and no one judges You, Your protege will not be humiliated, and the one you hate will not have the upper hand, You proclaim blessing and You remain transcendent, You can not escape only by taking refuge with you. "

Dua Qunut 2

Version from the hadith reported by at-Tirmidhi of Imam 'Ali.

اللهم أعوذ برضاك من سخطك وبمعافاتك من عقوبتك وأعوذ بك منك لا أحصي ثناء عليك أنت كما أثنيت على نفسك

"Allahoumma inni a'oudhou biridaka min sakhatik wa bi mou'afatika min 'ouqoubatik, wa bika mink a'oudhou the ouhsi thana year' alayka, anta ala kama athnayta nafsik. "

"My Lord, I ask that your pleasure protect me against your discontent, that the security you offer will protect me from your punishment. I ask you to protect me against yourself, I can not praise you perfectly, you are as you presented yourself. "
