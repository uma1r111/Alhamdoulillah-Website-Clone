Ibrahimiyya Prayer

Is Salat Ibrahimiya Mandatory in Prayer?

No. Unlike the tachahhud which is obligatory in salat, ibrahimiya prayer is not a pillar of prayer. It is still very recommended and reciting it is only beneficial. To deprive oneself of it would be to be avaricious. What is more beautiful than praying about the prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam)? There are also 6 known formulas of salat ibrahimiya. For each of them, you have the original version in Arabic, then in phonetics and in English.

Ibrahimiya Prayer 1

This formula was used by the prophet (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam). This is the version of the hadith reported by Ahmed.

اللهم صل على محمد، وعلى أهل بيته، وعلى أزواجه وذريته كما صليت على آل إبراهيم، إنك حميد مجيد، وبارك على محمد، وعلى آل بيته، وعلى أزواجه ذريته؛ كما باركت على آل إبراهيم، إنك حميد مجيد

« Allahumma salli 'ala mouhammed, wa 'ala ahli baytih, wa 'ala azwajihi wa dhurriyatih, kama sallayta 'ala ali ibrahim, innaka hamidun madjid, wa barik 'ala muhammed, wa 'ala ali baytih, wa 'ala azwajihi wa dhurriyatih, kama barakta 'ala ali ibrahim, innaka hamidun madjid. »

« O Allah, pray to Muhammed, the people of his house, his wives and his descendants, as you prayed over the family of Ibrahim, You are the only one worthy of Praise, the Glorious. O Allah, blessed Muhammed, the people of his house, his wives and his descendants, as you have blessed the family of Ibrahim, You are the only one worthy of Praise, the Glorious. »

Ibrahimia Prayer 2

The most famous version, reported by al Bukhari and Muslim.

اللهم صل على محمد، وعلى آل محمد كما صليت على إبراهيم وعلى آل إبراهيم، إنك حميد مجيد، اللهم بارك على محمد، وعلى آل محمد كما باركت على إبراهيم، وعلى آل إبراهيم، إنك حميد مجيد

« Allahumma salli 'ala muhammedin wa 'ala ali muhammed, kama sallayta 'ala ibrahima wa 'ala ali ibrahim, innaka hamidun majid. Allahumma barik 'ala muhammedin wa 'ala ali muhammed, kama barakta 'ala ali ibrahim, innaka hamidun majid. »

« O Allah, pray to Muhammed and his family, as you prayed over the family of Ibrahim, You are the only one worthy of Praise, the Glorious. And blessed Muhammed and his family, as you have blessed the family of Ibrahim, You are the only one worthy of Praise, the Glorious. »

Ibrahimiyyah Prayer 3

In another version reported by Muslim:

اللهم صل على محمد النبي الأمي، وعلى آل محمد؛ كما صليت على آل إبراهيم، وبارك على محمد النبي الأمي وعلى آل محمد، كما باركت على آل إبراهيم في العالمين، إنك حميد مجيد

« Allahumma salli 'ala muhammedi nnabiyyil ummiy, wa 'ala ali muhammed, kama sallayta 'ala ali ibrahim, wa barik 'ala muhammedi nnabiyyil ummiy wa 'ala ali muhammed, kama barakta 'ala ali ibrahim, fil 'alamina innaka hamidun majid. »

« O Allah, pray to Muhammad, the illiterate prophet, and his family, as you prayed over Ibrahim's family, and blessed Muhammed, the illiterate prophet, and his family, as you blessed Ibrahim's family, in the Universe, You are the only one worthy of Praise, the Glorious. »

Prayer Ibrahimiya 4

Another version of al Bukhari:

اللهمّ صل على محمد عبدك ورسولك كما صليت على آل إبراهيم، وبارك على محمد عبدك ورسولك، وعلى آل محمد كما باركت على إبراهيم وعلى آل إبراهيم

« Allahumma salli 'ala muhammedin 'abdika wa rasulik, kama sallayta 'ala ibrahim, wa barik 'ala muhammedin 'abdika wa rasulik, wa 'ala ali muhammed, kama barakta 'ala ali ibrahim. »

« O Allah, pray to Muhammad, your servant and your messenger as you prayed over the family of Ibrahim, and blessed Muhammad, your servant and your messenger, as you have blessed Ibrahim's family. »

Ibrahimiya Prayer 5

Another version of al Bukhari and Muslim:

اللهم صل على محمد، وعلى أهل بيته، وعلى أزواجه وذريته كما صليت على آل إبراهيم، وبارك على محمد، وعلى آل بيته، وعلى أزواجه ذريته؛ كما باركت على آل إبراهيم، إنك حميد مجيد

« Allahumma salli 'ala muhammed, wa 'ala ahli baytih, wa 'ala azwajihi wa dhurriyatih, kama sallayta 'ala ali ibrahim, wa barik 'ala muhammed, wa 'ala ali baytih, wa 'ala azwajihi wa dhurriyatih, kama barakta 'ala ali ibrahim, innaka hamidun madjid. »

« O Allah, pray to Muhammad, the people of his house, his wives and his descendants, as you prayed over the family of Ibrahim, and blessed Muhammad, the people of his house, his wives and his descendants, as you blessed the Ibrahim's family, You are the only one worthy of Praise, the Glorious. »

Ibrahimiya Prayer 6

This version is reported by an-Nasai:

اللهم صلّ على محمد، وعلى آل محمد، وبارك على محمد ، وعلى آل محمد، كما صليت وباركت على إبراهيم وآل إبراهيم، إنك حميد مجيد

« Allahumma salli 'ala muhammedin wa 'ala ali muhammed, wa barik 'ala muhammedin wa 'ala ali muhammed, kama sallayta wa barakta 'ala ali ibrahim, innaka hamidun majid. »

« O Allah, pray to Muhammed and his family, and bless Muhammad and his family, as you prayed and blessed the family of Ibrahim, You are the only one worthy of Praise, the Glorious. »
