Fajr Prayer

How to make Sobh's prayer?

Fajr and sobh, it's the same thing. In fact, what makes our brothers and sisters confused is that before the prayer of fajr (dawn), it is strongly recommended to pray 2 units (after the adhan, but before the iqama). Some people wrongly say that the Sunna prayer is fajr and the makeup is soubh. Many are complicated life while the case is simple. Even in the Prophet's hadiths (sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam), sometimes the word fadjr is used to refer to the obligatory morning prayer, sometimes it is the word soubh. So the obligatory morning prayer, you can call it al fadjr or as-soubh, as you want. This is not the goal here, but know in any case that the merits of this prayer are very numerous.

When to pray al Fajr, and until when?

Fajr's prayer begins from sunrise and ends until sunrise. There is divergence on the method of calculating dawn. The calculation angles used vary from 12 to 18 degrees. That's why, in order to suit everyone, our prayer time calendar tells you the different angles.

How to pray al Fajr?

The prayer of fajr is composed of 2 rakat. A rak'a is a unit of prayer. As we have seen, prayer has obligations and recommendations. As a reminder, some sounna of prayer were put in green.

Rak'a 1

  • Plan to pray al Fajr
  • Stand up, raise your hands and say "allahou akbar"
  • Put your right hand on your left hand
  • Call upon Allah
  • Say in a low voice "a'oudhoubillahi mina shaytani arradjim"
  • Recite the fatiha ( surah 1 ) then say "amine"
  • Recite what you want from the Quran
  • Say "allahou akbar" then bow
    • Say "soubhana rabbi al adhim" as many times as you want
  • Straighten the tilt saying "sami'allahou liman hamidah" ​​
  • Once standing, say "rabbana wa laka al hamd"
  • Say "allahou akbar" then bow down
    • Say "soubhana rabbi al a'la" as many times as you want
  • Make douas, invoke Allah during prostration
  • Say "allahu akbar" as you rise from prostration
  • Sit down for a moment and say "rabbi ghfirli"
  • Say "Allah" and then bow again
    • Say "soubhana rabbi al a'la" as many times as you want
  • Make douas, invoke Allah during prostration
  • Say "allahu akbar" as you rise from prostration
  • Take a stand and say "allahou akbar"

Rak'a 2

  • Once standing, redone as for the 1st rak'a until the 2nd prostration
  • As you fall from the 2nd prostration, sit still
  • Recite the tashahhoud and the ibrahimiya prayer
  • Turn your head to the right and say "assalamou alaykoum wa rahmatoullah"
  • Turn your head to the left and say "assalamou alaykoum wa rahmatoullah"
