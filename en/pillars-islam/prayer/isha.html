Isha Prayer

How to Do Isha's prayer?

Just like Dohr and al 'Asr , the Isha (evening) prayer consists of 4 rakat (prayer units). It's the same as Dohr and Asr, except that the first 2 rakat are loud and not in a low voice.

When to pray al Isha, and until when?

The prayer of Isha begins from the disappearance of the twilight until the middle of the night, or just before dawn.

How to pray al Isha?

The salat of Isha is identical to Dohr and Asr, with the first 2 rakat jahran (aloud). Also, the recitation of the Qur'an after surah al fatiha in the first 2 units is longer.
